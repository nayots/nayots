Audience: recruiters, engineers and developers arriving from a repo, CV or LinkedIn. Job: decide in seconds who this is and click through to real work. ## Direction contract

THESIS: The profile is one night sky. Every image is a plate cut from the same navy sky, so scrolling reads as one continuous observation, not a stack of widgets. Refuses the category default: centered "Hi there" heading, badge wall, three third-party stats cards.

OWN-WORLD: Flat navy #04060D plates, 840-unit viewBox, 20-unit radius, nebula cores (#141C3A) only away from the marks. Three star classes on every plate: dust (ice-white dots), stars (star-tone), glints (gold four-point stars echoing the keystone). Space Grotesk: 700 lowercase -0.035em for headings in ice-white; 500 for body in ice-white at reduced opacity; gold-highlight only for actions and glints. Hairline constellation lines in ice-accent at low opacity.

STORY: Visitor sees the name as stardust, learns breadth (.NET, AWS, front end, infrastructure, AI) as constellations, sees two real pieces of work, then the live year of activity as a sky; leaves via nayots.com or LinkedIn.
